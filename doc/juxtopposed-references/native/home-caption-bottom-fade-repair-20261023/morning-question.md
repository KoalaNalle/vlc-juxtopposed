# Caption layout question for the morning

When playback controls are visible, should captions temporarily move above
them, or retain their original position until the controls hide?

The original Figma player and hidden-controls frames keep the video behind
floating controls. They do not specify caption clearance. Small windows can
cover the lowest caption line. Saved subtitle offsets and authored ASS positions
must remain meaningful; neither should silently become a saved preference.

This is an open design preference, not a permission requirement. The redundant
legacy bottom fade can be removed independently in the redesigned player.
